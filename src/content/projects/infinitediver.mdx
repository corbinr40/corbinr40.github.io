<div className="section-wrapper">
  <FeatureSection title="What One Week Taught Me">
    <div className="textContainer">
      <p>Game jams are a particular kind of pressure cooker: you have to ship something, you don't have time to get precious, and every hour spent perfecting one thing is an hour not spent on another. A few things stuck with me:</p>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li>Scope down three times before you start building. Then do it again on day three.</li>
      <li>Placeholder art ships. I used coloured rectangles for most of the first four days and only replaced them once the mechanic actually worked.</li>
      <li>The "good enough" bar for a jam is not the same as the bar for a portfolio piece, and pretending otherwise is how you end up with nothing submitted.</li>
      <li>Playtesting your own game at 2am is how bugs get released.</li>
    </ul>
    <div className="textContainer">
      <p>Would I build Infinite Diver again? Yes. Would it look nothing like this one? Also yes.</p>
    </div>
